Slides for **`posit::conf(2026) %>% glimpse()`**, a talk by Mansi and Dionne for R-Ladies+ Melbourne.

## About the talk

Dionne and Mansi attended [posit::conf(2026)](https://posit.co/conference/) in Houston, Texas, as Opportunity Scholars. This talk isn't only about the conference. It's about the communities, relationships and contributions that made the opportunity possible.

They cover:

- their journeys through open source, community building, mentoring, advocacy and volunteering, and how that involvement opened unexpected doors
- how people find their way into international conferences, scholarships and global professional networks
- why networking and meaningful professional connections matter, and how contributing to communities creates impact for yourself and others
- favourite moments from the conference, lessons from meeting people across the global data science ecosystem, and insights that stayed with them afterwards

The talk is for anyone who is new to the R community, thinking about a first conference application, interested in open source, or curious about building a more connected, community-focused career.

All conference talks will be out on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@PositPBC) in December!
